# The Plan Behind TakeMeter

## Community: What community did you choose and why? Why is this community a good fit for a classification task — what makes the discourse varied enough to be interesting?
- The community I chose was r/WorldCup from Reddit! I chose this community for it's current relevance and because it is something that I am passionate about. This community is a good fit for a classification task because comments left on this subreddit contain a wide range of topics ranging from opinions, data, and live reactions to the games. What makes this discourse varied enough to be interesting is that there are a total of 48 countries competing in this tournament, therefore there is potential for millions of people to leave their ideas behind on r/WorldCup.

## Labels: What are your 2–4 labels? Define each in a complete sentence. Include 2 example posts per label.
1. Analysis - the post makes a structured argument backed by statistics, historical comparison, or tactical observation and the evidence is specific and verifiable.
    - Ex 1: Haaland has started this tournament exactly the way Norway needed him to. Two matches, four goals, and every opponent already looks terrified of giving him even half a chance. Norway's attack has pace, confidence, and a clear focal point.
    - Ex 2: Lionel Messi scores against Austria to overtake Klose and become the highest goal scorer in WC history
    - Mixed Ex 3: 5 subs and 3 breaks (half time and two quarter times) to make tactical adjustments. Yes, squad is becoming more important than star power.
2. Reaction - the post is driven by emotions and real time reactions to events. 
    - Ex 1: Senegal have been disappointing so far, expected more from the real African champions.
    - Ex 2: Feels bad that Senegal got put in the same group as France and Norway.
    - Mixed Ex 3: Haaland is a beast. Looking forward to the Norway vs France match.
3. Hot-Take - A bold claim that may be true but it is made without evidence.
    - Ex 1: Iran team is giving masterclass in composure at World Cup.
    - Ex 2: Senegal would have done well in other groups. Hope they still make it through to the round of 32
    - Mixed Ex 3: After their amazing run in 2018, Belgium has been declining, now i think this is the worst performance i've seen for Belgium since 2018 Is this the end for Belgium?

## Hard edge cases: What type of post will be genuinely ambiguous between two labels? How will you handle it when you encounter it during annotation?
- The type of posts that will genuinely be ambiguous between two labels will be posts that mix results/analysis with emotions/reactions. For example, a post made as a reaction to learning that their favorite team has only won a WC game when it is night time might be difficult to label because they are reacting emotionally while giving statistical evidence.
- In a case like the one just mentioned, I believe that it would be best to handle this issue by looking at what the driving factor is. In this case, the emotion is driven by the statistical fact, therefore the label should be `Analysis`.

## Data collection plan: Where will you collect examples? How many per label? What will you do if a label is underrepresented after 200 examples?
- I will collect examples primarily from Reddit (r/WorldCup). I will attempt to collect an even amount of each label, or at least 50 from a the label with the least amount of entries. If I am unable to find at least 50 examples of the most underrepresented label, I will delete it and fill the rest with a 50/50 split of the 2 other labels. 

## Evaluation metrics: Which metrics will you use to evaluate your model and why are those the right ones for this specific task? (Accuracy alone is not enough — explain what else you need and why.)
- The metrics I will use to evaluate the model are `Overall Accuracy` and `Per-Class Accuracy`. Accuracy alone is not enough because in the case the model learns to only identify 2/3 categories, the accuracy of 66% will not tell the full story, where one-third of the data will always not be learned right. A Per-Class Accuracy score will make this scenario identifiable. 

## Definition of success: What performance would make this classifier genuinely useful? What would you accept as "good enough" for deployment in a real community tool?
- A performace score of about 90% real accuracy would make this classifier genuinly useful. This would allow for a filtration system in a social-media type of app where users can filter real comments by data, reactions, or opinions.
